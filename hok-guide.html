READY FOR BATTLE

Your First Match

Before entering the battlefield, keep these simple tips in mind to help you play smarter and support your team.

🪙
TIP 01

Farm Before Fighting

Clear minions and earn gold to buy equipment. Avoid rushing into fights when you are underpowered.

👀
TIP 02

Watch the Mini-Map

Check the mini-map regularly to keep track of teammates, enemies and possible danger.

🤝
TIP 03

Stay With Your Team

Avoid fighting multiple enemies alone. Stay close to your teammates during important battles.

🏰
TIP 04

Focus on Towers

Kills are useful, but destroying towers and the enemy crystal is how your team wins the match.

🐉
TIP 05

Help With Objectives

Join your teammates when they are taking important objectives to gain an advantage for your team.

🎮
TIP 06

Learn From Every Match

Don't worry about making mistakes. Practise your heroes, learn from each game and keep improving.
