CLASSIC

Classic is the standard 5v5 game mode in Mobile Legends. Players choose their heroes and battle against the enemy team across three lanes.

It is a great mode for practising heroes, testing builds and learning different strategies without affecting your Ranked position.

RANKED

Ranked is the competitive 5v5 mode where players compete to increase their rank.

Winning matches helps you climb through the ranks, while losing can cause you to lose rank points or stars depending on your current rank.
