Guide of Honor of Kings and Mobile Legend

Here’s a clear introduction and guide to Honor of Kings and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, with tips to help you understand and enjoy both games.

Honor of King

Honor of Kings is the world's most-played mobile MOBA, offering fast 5v5 battles, a huge roster of legendary heroes, and a thriving esports scene. It was first released in China in 2015 and launched globally in June 2024, quickly becoming the highest-grossing mobile game worldwide

Mobile Legend

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) is one of the most downloaded mobile MOBAs ever, with over 1.5 billion installations worldwide and around 42 million monthly active players. Released in 2016 by Moonton, it has become a global esports phenomenon, especially in Southeast Asia, and continues to break viewership records at its annual World Championships.
