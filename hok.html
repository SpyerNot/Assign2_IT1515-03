What is Honor of Kings?

Honor of Kings is a multiplayer online battle arena game where two teams of five players compete against each other. Each player selects a hero with unique abilities and works together with teammates to destroy the enemy's towers and crystal.

The game combines action, strategy, communication, and teamwork. Players can choose different roles depending on their preferred playstyle, such as dealing damage, protecting teammates, or controlling important objectives.
